Jump Slots

TabJump Help

TabJump gives you temporary jump slots for tabs you are using right now.

Use the popup

The key will open the popup.

  • Jump switches to the tab assigned to that slot.
  • Assign assigns the current tab to that slot.
  • Clear All removes all current assignments.

Your Firefox shortcut settings appear to differ from these defaults.

Notes

  • Assignments are temporary. Closed tabs lose their slots.
  • Tree Style Tab users can enable slot badges in Options.
